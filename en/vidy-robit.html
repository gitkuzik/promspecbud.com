Driven reinforced-concrete piles

Installation of pile foundations from driven reinforced-concrete precast piles with cross-sections of 300×300 mm, 350×350 mm, 400×400 mm, up to 15 m long as single elements, and composite piles up to 24 m long.

The use of high-performance Junttan, BSP and BRUCE hydraulic hammers with adjustable impact energy ensures the integrity of the pile head, no longitudinal cracks and maximum work speed. Our company achieves record productivity for the Ukrainian market — up to 5,000 m³ of piles per month.

Technical specifications:

  • Pile cross-sections: 300×300, 350×350, 400×400 mm
  • Length: from 4 to 15 m (single), up to 24 m (composite with welded joint)
  • Equipment fleet: 4 pile-driving units with hydraulic hammers, 5 SP-49, SP-47, EO-5111 pile drivers
  • Applications: elevators, terminals, plants, logistics complexes, residential areas

Bored piles (RH-25)

Installation of bored piles with diameters from 600 to 1200 mm and depths up to 28 m using the German O&K RH-25 drilling rig with a BB-10 drilling head.

The technology is used under significant vertical and horizontal loads (bridge piers, viaducts, retaining walls, heavy metallurgical foundations). Drilling can be performed under the protection of temporary casing pipes or bentonite slurry, which completely eliminates borehole wall collapse in water-saturated soils.

Technical specifications:

  • Drilling diameters: 600, 800, 1000, 1200 mm
  • Installation depth: up to 28 m
  • Torque: 250 kNm
  • Bearing capacity per pile: up to 300–500+ tonnes

Continuous flight auger (CFA)

CFA piles (Bauer RG-20)

A modern complex consisting of the German multifunctional BAUER RG-20 rig with a WG-8 drilling head and a high-performance stationary Schwing BPL 160-HD concrete pump.

CFA technology allows continuous drilling with a hollow auger while concreting under excess pressure as the auger is withdrawn. After the borehole is filled with concrete, a spatial reinforcement cage is lowered into the fresh mix using a vibrator. This eliminates soil loosening and ensures flawless pile shaft quality.

Key advantages:

  • No vibrations or shock waves (safe for adjacent buildings)
  • High speed: up to 20–30 piles per shift with one crew
  • Strengthening of damaged foundations and adding storeys

Leader-hole drilling

Drilling of advance (leader) guide holes 200–350 mm in diameter and up to 12–16 m deep before driving piles.

Pre-drilling is essential in dense sandy, clayey or fill soils containing construction debris. It removes excessive soil resistance during driving, prevents chipping and breakage of reinforced-concrete piles, reduces seismic impact on nearby buildings and guarantees vertical installation.

Technical equipment:

  • SP-49 and SP-47 pile drivers with SO-2 auger drilling equipment
  • Precise alignment and fixing to design axes

DBN quality control

Static and dynamic pile testing

Certified pile load testing up to 400+ tonnes in accordance with DBN V.2.1-10:2018 and DSTU B V.2.1-27:2010.

Static testing is the most accurate method for determining the bearing capacity of soils and piles. We have our own steel reaction beam frames, anchor systems, calibrated hydraulic jacks and digital deformation-measuring indicators. Timely testing at an early stage allows designers to optimize pile lengths and save up to 20–30% of the foundation cost.

The service includes:

  • Dynamic testing: measuring refusal during driving and after a 'rest' period
  • Static testing: stepped loading with settlement stabilization recording
  • Official technical report: settlement charts, conclusions for DABI

Eco-friendly and silent

Pile pressing (Fundex / Starke)

Vibration-free pile installation using Fundex (in a casing with a lost tip) and Starke (static pressing with up to 320 tonnes of force) technologies.

The technology is indispensable for infill development in city centres, near architectural landmarks, residential buildings, hospitals or underground utilities. The process is fully controlled by pressure gauges, guaranteeing the design bearing capacity for each pile.

Key parameters:

  • Pressing force: up to 3,200 kN (320 tonnes)
  • No dynamic shock or noise
  • Can install both reinforced-concrete piles and steel pipes

Diaphragm wall & ground anchors

Construction of load-bearing and water-tight retaining walls using the diaphragm wall method to a depth of 25 m, as well as installation of temporary and permanent ground anchors.

It ensures absolute safety when constructing underground car parks and basements deeper than 6–10 m and protects neighbouring foundations from settlement. Ground anchors transfer thrust loads from the sheet-pile wall into deep stable soil, freeing the excavation from internal struts.
