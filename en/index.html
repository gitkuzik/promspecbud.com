About the company

Promspecbud LLC, Kremenchuk

The company was founded on 11 March 2003 under the permanent leadership of director Viktor Kuzik. Over more than two decades we have grown from a local crew into one of the most powerful specialized foundation contractors in Ukraine.

Technical capabilities

Piling and foundation services

We deliver the full scope of zero-cycle works and deep foundation construction in any geological conditions across Ukraine.

Core specialization

Driven piles (hydraulic hammers)

Driving of reinforced-concrete precast piles with 300×300, 350×350, 400×400 mm cross-sections, solid up to 15 m and spliced up to 24 m. Record speed thanks to Junttan and Bruce hydraulic hammers.

  • Cross-section: 300, 350, 400 mm; length up to 24 m
  • Up to 5,000 m³ of piles monthly by a single company
  • Safe, gentle impact regime for each pile
More
High bearing capacity

Bored piles (O&K RH-25)

Installation of large-diameter piles by auger or bucket drilling with casing. Ideal for bridge piers, heavy industrial structures and retaining walls.

  • O&K RH-25 drilling rig with BB-10 head
  • Diameters from 600 to 1200 mm, depth up to 28 m
  • Works in difficult, water-saturated soils
More
CFA technology

CFA piles (Bauer RG-20)

Pile construction using CFA technology (continuous flight auger) together with a powerful Schwing BPL 160-HD concrete pump. Delivers high speed with no vibration near neighboring buildings.

  • BAUER RG-20 rig with WG-8 head
  • Concrete mix supplied under pressure through the auger
  • Strengthening existing foundations and reconstruction
More
Difficult soils

Pre-drilling (leader holes)

Pre-drilling of guiding boreholes with SP-49 and SP-47 rigs equipped with SO-2 auger heads. Allows driven piles to pass through dense sand, loam or made ground without risk of pile damage.

  • Reduced dynamic loads on the soil
  • Prevents pile deviation from the design axis
  • Own fleet of mounted drilling equipment
More
Quality control

Pile testing (up to 400+ t)

Full-scale static and dynamic pile tests according to DBN and DSTU. Our own heavy test frames, hydraulic jacks, deflectometers and strain-gauge stations with official reports and settlement charts.

  • Determines the actual soil bearing capacity
  • Enables optimization of pile length and quantity
  • Certified instruments and a full technical report
More
Silent and vibration-free

Pile pressing (Fundex / Starke)

Pile installation by static pressing with up to 320 tonnes force, or pressing in a casing with a lost tip (Fundex). The only safe method right next to dilapidated or historic buildings.

  • Zero dynamic impact on the surroundings
  • Real-time control of pressing force
  • Ideal for dense urban development
More
Underground construction

Diaphragm wall & ground anchors

Construction of load-bearing and cut-off walls for deep excavations, underground parking and transport interchanges. Installation of temporary and permanent ground anchors to reliably fix sheet-pile walls.

  • Wall depth up to 25 meters
  • Reliable waterproofing and load-bearing barrier
  • High-strength anchor fixing
More
Turnkey

Monolithic pile caps & earthworks

Complete foundation preparation: excavation, soil removal and grading, mechanized pile-head trimming, reinforcement and concreting of massive monolithic pile caps for columns and silos.

  • Pile-head trimming without rebar damage
  • Full compliance with the design foundation geometry
  • Handover of the completed zero cycle to the client
