Exclusive technologies

Junttan, BSP and TM BRUCE hydraulic hammers

Promspecbud LLC (Kremenchuk) holds an exclusive dealer agreement with the leading South Korean manufacturer of hydraulic hammers, TM “BRUCE” (Powerquip).

Using modern hydraulic hammers instead of outdated tubular diesel hammers increases the pace of work 5 times. A hydraulic hammer delivers a controlled impulse without destructive peak loads on the pile, allowing it to be driven into the densest soils.

Key advantages of hydraulic hammers:

  • Adjustable impact energy: from 10% to 100% via a computer control panel
  • High blow rate: 40–100 blows/min (diesel only 38–42)
  • Eco-friendly and clean: no smoke or unburned diesel emissions
  • Pile protection: no concrete spalling or rebar damage
